How it works

From set logged to story posted

Hevy is where I log every set. This is everything I wanted on top of it.

1

Pull the data

Hevy's API copies every workout into a database I own. 115 workouts so far.

2

Crunch it

Estimated 1RMs, PRs, volume, sets per muscle group, streaks. It even flags sets that look like typos.

3

Make the cards

Every session becomes a black and green RIGZ card, sized for feed posts, stories and carousels.

4

Look back

A dashboard with a clickable training calendar, records, comparisons and weekly or monthly reports.
