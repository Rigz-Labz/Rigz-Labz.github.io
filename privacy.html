Privacy policy

Rigz Liftz (a Rigz Labz project) is a personal gym dashboard used only by its owner. It uses workout data from Hevy but is not affiliated with or endorsed by Hevy.

What it accesses

  • The owner's own workout data, through the Hevy API, using the owner's own API key.
  • In Google Drive, only the files and folders this tool creates itself (the drive.file permission). It cannot see or change anything else in the Drive.

What it does with it

It makes workout images and uploads them to the owner's own Google Drive. Nothing is sold, shared, or sent to anyone else, and no data about other people is collected.

Removing access

Access can be withdrawn at any time from the Google Account's Third-party connections page.
