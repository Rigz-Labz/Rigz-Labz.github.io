Privacy policy

Rigz Labz tools are personal tools used only by their owner. This policy covers both of them.

Rigz Liftz

A personal gym dashboard. It uses workout data from Hevy but is not affiliated with or endorsed by Hevy.

  • It reads the owner's own workout data through the Hevy API, using the owner's own API key.
  • In Google Drive, it can only see and change the files and folders it creates itself (the drive.file permission). It cannot see or change anything else in the Drive.
  • It makes workout images and uploads them to the owner's own Google Drive.

Rigz Arrowz

A personal archery dashboard. It uses data exported from Archery Success but is not affiliated with or endorsed by Archery Success.

  • It reads export files the owner copies to their own computer. It connects to no online accounts.
  • From the profile in the export it keeps only the archery category (bow, age group, gender) and weekly arrow target, never name, email, date of birth or club.

What happens to the data

Both tools keep their data on the owner's own computer. Nothing is sold, shared, or sent to anyone else, and no data about other people is collected.

Removing access

Rigz Liftz's Google Drive access can be withdrawn at any time from the Google Account's Third-party connections page.
